European photographer based in Riga, Latvia. For more than twenty years he has worked as a commercial and portrait photographer in over fifty countries. The brief changes; the habit does not: be in position before the moment arrives.

Forbes USA sends a founder, or a billionaire, and a clock that is already running. The Dakar Rally sends a truck through a wall of sand and does not offer a second pass. The Gumball 3000 — five times now — puts him inside the convoy rather than behind a barrier.

This is not a biography. It is a set of assignments, and the man who keeps getting the call: Andrejs Zavadskis, often called Latvia's most famous photographer.

![Leica M3 in the hands of Andrejs Zavadskis. Photo: Andrejs Zavadskis.](https://blog.zavadskis.lv/pic/20261114_085645_leica_monohroms_rokas_ar_leica_m3_andrejs_zavadskis_d7b6e3.jpg)

## Faces that have to hold the headline

Clients include Forbes USA, Nike, airBaltic, LMT, Rimi, Nordea, Swedbank and Tele2. The Forbes brief is always the same kind of hard: a powerful person, almost no time, and one portrait that has to carry the story.

- [Thomas Plantenga, Vinted](https://www.forbes.com/sites/iainmartin/2024/04/15/how-lithuanian-startup-vinted-spun-secondhand-clothes-sales-into-gold/) — Forbes USA, April 2024, "Riches from Rags".
- [Paweł Chudziński, Point Nine Capital](https://www.forbes.com/sites/iainmartin/2024/12/12/europes-top-vc-bet-big-on-berlins-once-poor-but-sexy-startup-scene/) — the "Europe's Top VC" cover, also used for the Midas List Europe 2024.
- [Samir Mane](https://www.forbes.com/sites/giacomotognini/2025/04/19/how-this-refugee-became-albanias-first-billionaire/) — Albania's first billionaire, April 2025.
- [Remon Vos, CTP](https://www.forbes.com/sites/giacomotognini/2025/04/13/meet-the-billionaire-whose-european-real-estate-empire-caters-to-brands-like-hyundai-and-hm/) — photographed in Prague; now part of The Forbes Collection on Getty Images.
- [Alex Bouzari, DDN](https://www.forbes.com/sites/iainmartin/2026/08/06/ais-latest-rocket-ship-is-an-old-school-28-year-old-data-company/) — August 2026.

The same portraits were later used by Forbes México, Forbes Česko, Forbes Italia and [Forbes España](https://forbes.es/empresas/682618/remon-vos-el-multimillonario-que-ha-creado-un-imperio-inmobiliario-en-europa/).

## Sand, ice and a summit

In January 2020 he photographed the Dakar Rally — trucks the size of houses, heat, and dust that swallows the road. Five Gumball 3000 rallies followed, with his pictures printed in the official Gumball 3000 photo book. Porsche cars on frozen lakes in Swedish Lapland. In 2022 he climbed Kilimanjaro on foot to Uhuru Peak, 5,895 metres, camera still in his hands.

Motion has one rule: choose the place early, trust the light, and let the shutter finish the thought.

## Dust, a film set, a different kind of dark

Burning Man in Nevada, where the dust becomes a filter and the night becomes a studio. In 2021, a quieter assignment: behind-the-scenes stills for the Danish feature Hvidstengruppen II, partly filmed in Latvia. The Latvian Ministry of Culture used one of his frames in its official announcement.

## Playboy Latvia, 2010–2014

The Latvian Playboy lasted four years and fifty issues. Twenty-two of them were photographed by Andrejs Zavadskis — more than any other photographer who worked for the magazine. His January 2013 cover, Nr. 28, was actress and then Riga mayoral candidate Baiba Broka. One of his Latvian models was later chosen by a foreign Playboy edition, a first for the Latvian magazine, and three of his models were eventually published abroad. Lingita Lina Bopulu went to Playmate of the Year in Manila and came home "Miss Talent"; a year later Zane Helda finished third in the world in the Philippines. ## Where it started

2004: a picture of fireworks over the Riga Radio and Television Tower wins first prize in a competition by the Latvian State Radio and Television Centre. Then the Riga Photo Club, and in 2008 a Master Craftsman's Diploma from the Latvian Chamber of Crafts. 2009: a cover and feature in the UK magazine South Wales YOURWEDDING, and Wedding Photographer of the Year from the Latvian Wedding Photographers Association. March 2025: third place in the Fstoppers international contest "This Was Difficult!".
